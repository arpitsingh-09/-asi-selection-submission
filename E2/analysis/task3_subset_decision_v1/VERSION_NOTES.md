# First-version checks

**Learning from Peers in Reasoning Models (qImzresc4C).** The first arXiv version was submitted 12 May 2025, before the October 2025 benchmark release. It presents the same LeaP and LeaP-T methods. The benchmark abstract adds a result for ZebraLogic that is absent from the May abstract. Binding the October abstract to the May date would assign at least one claim to the wrong text. Hold out pending evidence for the required version. [May abstract and submission history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07787v1)

**StreamingThinker (10Iiew095e).** The archived OpenReview release on 8 October 2025 precedes the 20 October arXiv submission. The arXiv record corroborates the work and supplies a later version. Earliest public release and the abstract at that release remain to be verified. [arXiv submission history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17238)

**Expanding Reasoning Potential in Foundation Model by Learning Diverse Chains of Thought Patterns (3FQV4JHPpY).** The 25 September 2025 arXiv submission precedes the October benchmark record. The matching title supplies a primary version to compare. The release alone leaves the work’s earliest history and benchmark text binding unresolved. [arXiv submission history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21124)

## Two further confirmed pre-bound works

**Multilevel Control Functional (Ahdsg2nkNH).** The arXiv version history starts 22 May 2023. The current primary record matches the benchmark title, all five archived authors, and the distinctive combination of Stein control variates, multi-fidelity estimation and Bayesian applications. Work-level identity and the version history establish the exclusion even though the latest abstract was revised. [Primary version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.12996)

**Surpassing Cosine Similarity (4i66VARUHD).** The 11 July 2024 first version names Federico Tessari and Neville Hogan. Both authors recur in the benchmark, which adds Kunpeng Yao. Both abstracts introduce DIEM to address the dimension dependence of cosine similarity and describe its stable variability across dimensions. The benchmark changes the examples. The earlier work meets the exclusion rule. [First version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08623v1)
