# Task 3 cohort decision

On 18 November 2026, acting on the applicant’s request to resolve the cohort decision, Codex selected the separately reported subset described in the prospective amendment. This is a delegated design decision by Codex. Independent human verification and numerical execution have their own records.

The original 299-work analysis cannot meet the proposed chronological criterion. Four documented works were publicly available by the end of 2024. Preserve that outcome for the original assignment cohort. For the supplementary analysis, require positive evidence of first public release strictly after 2024-12-31T23:59:59.999999Z and evidence that the archived abstract used for scoring was available at that release. The date is a conservative upper bound on when the pinned weights already existed. It does not establish the last publication date in their training data.

Apply the same rule to all 299 works regardless of label or claim availability. Exclude confirmed earlier works. Hold unresolved histories out of the executable cohort. Compare work identity across title changes, authors, methods and versions. A dated preprint after the bound identifies a release to investigate. A title match or an unsuccessful search alone cannot establish the earliest release. If an earlier abstract differs, record the mismatch before any decision about changing the scored text.

Before execution, freeze the eligible IDs, date and text evidence, chronological reference pool, source-bound claims, code and embeddings. Verify both outcome classes and preserve the original quality baseline, score orientations and paired bootstrap design. Prior benchmark exposure remains disclosed. A run on any resulting subset must report its denominator and exclusions.
