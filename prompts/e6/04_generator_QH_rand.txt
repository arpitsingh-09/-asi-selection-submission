Next, we’ll prepare Template 04 — QH-rand (Random Question Selection).

Purpose

This arm follows the same process as QH-sel, except that the three questions are selected randomly rather than by ranking.

To ensure a fair comparison:

* Both arms receive the exact same six candidate questions.
* QH-sel receives the three highest-ranked distinct questions.
* QH-rand receives three randomly selected questions.
* Both generate exactly two hypotheses per selected question.

Run:

Step 2 — Paste this prompt

ROLE

You are an AI research scientist. Your task is to generate testable hypotheses from three randomly selected research questions.

INPUT

Research domain and task:
{{DOMAIN_AND_TASK}}

Research evidence:
{{EVIDENCE}}

Three randomly selected research questions:
{{SELECTED_QUESTIONS}}

INSTRUCTIONS

1. Use exactly the three supplied research questions.
2. These questions have already been selected randomly by the experiment script.
3. Do not rank, score, replace, modify, or reorder the questions.
4. For EACH research question, generate exactly TWO distinct hypotheses.
5. Generate exactly SIX hypotheses overall.

HYPOTHESIS REQUIREMENTS

Each hypothesis must:

* Be one declarative sentence.
* Contain no more than 50 words.
* Express a specific, testable relationship, mechanism, or prediction.
* Directly address its associated research question.
* Be grounded in the supplied research evidence.
* Be meaningfully different from its paired hypothesis.

RESTRICTIONS

* Do not generate additional research questions.
* Do not invent evidence.
* Do not claim experimental validation.
* Do not provide explanations, introductions, conclusions, citations, or recommendations.

OUTPUT FORMAT

Return valid JSON only:

{
“arm”: “QH-rand”,
“questions”: [
{
“id”: “Q1”,
“text”: “First randomly selected research question.”,
“hypotheses”: [
{“id”: “H1”, “text”: “First hypothesis.”},
{“id”: “H2”, “text”: “Second hypothesis.”}
]
},
{
“id”: “Q2”,
“text”: “Second randomly selected research question.”,
“hypotheses”: [
{“id”: “H3”, “text”: “Third hypothesis.”},
{“id”: “H4”, “text”: “Fourth hypothesis.”}
]
},
{
“id”: “Q3”,
“text”: “Third randomly selected research question.”,
“hypotheses”: [
{“id”: “H5”, “text”: “Fifth hypothesis.”},
{“id”: “H6”, “text”: “Sixth hypothesis.”}
]
}
]
}

FINAL VALIDATION

Verify that:

* Exactly three supplied questions are used.
* Exactly two hypotheses are generated per question.
* Exactly six hypotheses are present.
* Each hypothesis contains no more than 50 words.
* All hypotheses are distinct and testable.
* The response is valid JSON with no additional text.
