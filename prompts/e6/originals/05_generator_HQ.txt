Next is Template 05 — HQ (Hypothesis → Question).

Purpose

This arm reverses the QH process:

1. Generate exactly six hypotheses from the research evidence.
2. Generate one research question for each hypothesis.
3. Each question must explain what its corresponding hypothesis attempts to investigate.

Step 2 — Paste this prompt

ROLE

You are an AI research scientist. Your task is to generate research hypotheses first and then formulate research questions that correspond to those hypotheses.

INPUT

Research domain and task:
{{DOMAIN_AND_TASK}}

Research evidence:
{{EVIDENCE}}

INSTRUCTIONS

STAGE 1 — HYPOTHESIS GENERATION

1. Examine the research task and supporting evidence.
2. Generate exactly SIX distinct research hypotheses.
3. Each hypothesis must:
    * Be one declarative sentence.
    * Contain no more than 50 words.
    * Express a specific, testable relationship, mechanism, or prediction.
    * Be relevant to the research task.
    * Be grounded in the supplied evidence.
    * Be meaningfully distinct from the other hypotheses.

STAGE 2 — RESEARCH QUESTION GENERATION

4. For EACH hypothesis, generate exactly ONE research question.
5. Each question must:
    * Directly investigate its corresponding hypothesis.
    * Be clear, specific, and researchable.
    * Preserve the central relationship or mechanism in the hypothesis.
    * Be meaningfully different from the other five questions.
6. Do not generate additional hypotheses or questions.

GENERAL RESTRICTIONS

* Do not invent evidence.
* Do not claim experimental validation.
* Do not generate hypotheses by copying the same idea with different wording.
* Do not provide explanations, introductions, conclusions, citations, or recommendations.

OUTPUT FORMAT

Return valid JSON only:

{
“arm”: “HQ”,
“hypotheses”: [
{
“id”: “H1”,
“text”: “First hypothesis.”,
“research_question”: “First research question.”
},
{
“id”: “H2”,
“text”: “Second hypothesis.”,
“research_question”: “Second research question.”
},
{
“id”: “H3”,
“text”: “Third hypothesis.”,
“research_question”: “Third research question.”
},
{
“id”: “H4”,
“text”: “Fourth hypothesis.”,
“research_question”: “Fourth research question.”
},
{
“id”: “H5”,
“text”: “Fifth hypothesis.”,
“research_question”: “Fifth research question.”
},
{
“id”: “H6”,
“text”: “Sixth hypothesis.”,
“research_question”: “Sixth research question.”
}
]
}

FINAL VALIDATION

Verify that:

* Exactly six hypotheses are present.
* Exactly one research question corresponds to each hypothesis.
* Every hypothesis is one declarative sentence of no more than 50 words.
* Every question directly investigates its corresponding hypothesis.
* All hypotheses and questions are meaningfully distinct.
* The response is valid JSON with no additional text.
