Next is Template 06 — Control Conditions (D-empty and D-shuffled).

Purpose

We need two control conditions to understand whether the quality of evidence affects hypothesis generation.

* D-empty: The model receives the research domain and task but no substantive research evidence.
* D-shuffled: The model receives evidence belonging to a different research input.

Both conditions generate exactly six hypotheses.

Step 1 — Create the file

Run:

touch prompts/e6/06_generator_controls.txt
open -e prompts/e6/06_generator_controls.txt

Step 2 — Paste this prompt

ROLE

You are an AI research scientist. Your task is to generate testable research hypotheses under a predefined experimental control condition.

INPUT

Experimental arm:
{{ARM_NAME}}

Research domain and task:
{{DOMAIN_AND_TASK}}

Evidence:
{{EVIDENCE}}

Control condition:
{{CONTROL_CONDITION_INSTRUCTIONS}}

INSTRUCTIONS

1. Follow the supplied control-condition instructions exactly.
2. Generate exactly SIX distinct research hypotheses.
3. Each hypothesis must:
    * Be one declarative sentence.
    * Contain no more than 50 words.
    * Express a specific, testable relationship, mechanism, or prediction.
    * Be relevant to the research domain and task.
    * Be meaningfully distinct from the other five hypotheses.
4. Use only the information available in the input.
5. Do not invent evidence or claim experimental validation.
6. Do not generate research questions.
7. Do not provide explanations, introductions, conclusions, citations, or recommendations.

OUTPUT FORMAT

Return valid JSON only:

{
“arm”: “{{ARM_NAME}}”,
“hypotheses”: [
{“id”: “H1”, “text”: “First hypothesis.”},
{“id”: “H2”, “text”: “Second hypothesis.”},
{“id”: “H3”, “text”: “Third hypothesis.”},
{“id”: “H4”, “text”: “Fourth hypothesis.”},
{“id”: “H5”, “text”: “Fifth hypothesis.”},
{“id”: “H6”, “text”: “Sixth hypothesis.”}
]
}

FINAL VALIDATION

Verify that:

* The arm name matches the supplied experimental arm.
* Exactly six hypotheses are present.
* Each hypothesis is one declarative sentence.
* Each hypothesis contains no more than 50 words.
* All six hypotheses are meaningfully distinct.
* The response is valid JSON with no additional text.

Step 3 — Important control instructions

Your Python script must supply the appropriate instructions.

For D-empty:

No substantive research evidence is provided. Generate hypotheses using only the research domain and task. Do not pretend that supporting evidence was supplied.

For D-shuffled:

The supplied evidence belongs to a different research input. Generate hypotheses for the current research task using the supplied evidence as presented. Do not assume that the evidence belongs to the current task.
