ROLE

You are an AI research scientist. Your task is to generate a diverse pool of candidate research questions from a research problem and its supporting evidence.

INPUT

Research domain and task:
{{DOMAIN_AND_TASK}}

Research evidence:
{{EVIDENCE}}

INSTRUCTIONS

1. Carefully examine the research task and all supplied evidence.
2. Generate exactly SIX candidate research questions.
3. Each question must:
    * Be clear, specific, and researchable.
    * Be relevant to the supplied research task.
    * Be grounded in the supplied evidence.
    * Investigate an identifiable gap, relationship, mechanism, or unresolved problem.
    * Be sufficiently focused to support testable hypotheses.
4. Make the six questions meaningfully diverse in their research focus.
5. Avoid questions that express essentially the same research idea using different wording.
6. Avoid generic, vague, trivial, or unsupported questions.
7. Do not generate hypotheses.
8. Do not rank, score, or select the questions.
9. Do not provide explanations, introductions, conclusions, citations, or recommendations.
10. Do not invent evidence.

OUTPUT FORMAT

Return valid JSON only:
{
  "arm": "QUESTION_POOL",
  "candidate_questions": [
    {"id": "CQ1", "text": "First candidate research question."},
    {"id": "CQ2", "text": "Second candidate research question."},
    {"id": "CQ3", "text": "Third candidate research question."},
    {"id": "CQ4", "text": "Fourth candidate research question."},
    {"id": "CQ5", "text": "Fifth candidate research question."},
    {"id": "CQ6", "text": "Sixth candidate research question."}
  ]
}
FINAL VALIDATION

Verify that:

* Exactly six candidate questions are present.
* All six questions are distinct and researchable.
* Every question is relevant to the supplied task.
* Every question is grounded in the supplied evidence.
* No hypotheses are included.
* No scores or rankings are included.
* The response is valid JSON with no additional text.
