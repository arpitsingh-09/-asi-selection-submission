ROLE

You are an AI research scientist. Your task is to identify testable research hypotheses from a research problem and its supporting evidence.

INPUT

Research domain and task:
{{DOMAIN_AND_TASK}}

Research evidence:
{{EVIDENCE}}

INSTRUCTIONS

1. Carefully examine the research task and all supplied evidence.
2. Identify important patterns, gaps, relationships, mechanisms, or unresolved problems.
3. Generate exactly SIX distinct research hypotheses directly from the input.
4. Each hypothesis must:
    * Be one declarative sentence.
    * Contain no more than 50 words.
    * Express a specific, testable relationship, mechanism, or prediction.
    * Be relevant to the supplied research task.
    * Be grounded in the supplied evidence.
    * Be meaningfully distinct from the other five hypotheses.
5. Avoid vague claims, generic observations, unsupported assumptions, and simple restatements of the input.
6. Do not generate research questions.
7. Do not provide explanations, introductions, conclusions, citations, or recommendations.
8. Do not invent evidence or claim that a hypothesis has been experimentally validated.

Return valid JSON only, using exactly this structure:

      "id": "H6",

    }
  ]
}
FINAL VALIDATION

Before returning the result, verify that:

* Exactly six hypotheses are present.
* Every hypothesis is one declarative sentence.
* Every hypothesis contains no more than 50 words.
* No hypothesis is a research question.
* All six hypotheses are distinct.
* The response is valid JSON with no additional text.
