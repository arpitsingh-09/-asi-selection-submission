# Draft Amendment 003 — Partial supplementary evaluation without decoy control

**Status: DRAFT FOR REVIEW — not approved, committed, or authorization to score.**
Applies only to the supplementary A1 replication; it does not amend original
registered A1 or authorize scoring.

## Proposed scope and controls

Retain the frozen 50-paper cohort and all source, extraction, validation, and
decoy evidence unchanged. Keep the original-pool, empty-pool, and shuffled-pool
conditions. Mark the same-topic decoy condition **unavailable for every focal**
unless and until an eligible candidate is independently verified under an
approved protocol. Do not substitute, rank, or score replacement decoys.
Preserve the original proposals and review records; do not impute decoy results.
Empty-pool scores remain undefined.

The existing packet contains 43 reviewed focal records and 217 extracted
claims. The saved Copilot suggestions are AI-assisted triage only, not human
verification. Three proposed decoys postdate their focal papers; the remaining
candidates still lack the required independent topical judgment and
verification that all reused pool papers predate the decoy. Amendment 002 also
records one temporally passing pair that is not established as topically
suitable. These limitations and source records must remain in the audit trail;
no candidate is currently verified as an eligible decoy.

## Analyses and interpretation

Without the decoy condition, the following preregistered contrasts remain
computable for focal records with complete, validated inputs and required
scores:

- Partial-coverage contrasts: original-pool reference versus 25% coverage,
  then adjacent 25% → 50% → 75% → 100% levels.
- Concentration contrast: distributed versus concentrated claim placement.
- Diagnostic comparisons of original and shuffled pools. Report empty-pool
  outputs as undefined, not as numeric scores.

Keep the registered paired-by-focal bootstrap and report all planned
coordinates and undefinedness. Exclude incomplete records from the relevant
contrast and report exclusions; do not replace focal papers. No decoy-condition
effect or decoy-based conclusion is estimable. Any pass criterion can apply
only to the retained partial-coverage and concentration contrasts as written;
it cannot establish completion of the full control set.

This is an **exposed, partial supplementary replication** of the specified
contrasts under the E2 pool operationalization. It is not an unchanged,
complete, blinded, or independent execution of original A1, and must not be
reported as one. Original A1 registration and results remain distinct and
unchanged. This amendment proposes a deviation from the supplementary
registration's missing-required-condition rule and requires explicit approval
and a separate registration commit before scoring.

## Minimum claim-review gate before scoring

Before any focal paper is scored, one independent human reviewer who did not
produce the extraction must review every extracted claim for that focal against
its original abstract, record a semantic-support judgment and an atomicity
judgment with a short rationale, and resolve or exclude unsupported, uncertain,
or compound claims. The review must be recorded with reviewer identity/code
and date; AI-assisted suggestions do not satisfy this requirement. Every focal
included in scoring must then meet the registered 4–10 distinct atomic-claim
rule, have exact supporting abstract excerpts for each retained claim, and
complete source/validation records. Otherwise exclude that focal from scoring
and log the reason without replacing it.

**No approval, commit, inference, manipulation evaluation, or novelty scoring
is authorized by this draft.**
